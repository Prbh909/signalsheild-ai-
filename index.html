How SignalShield works

A simple AI-assisted workflow designed for learning and human review.

01

Collect input

Type a message, record speech, or select an audio clip to transcribe.

02

Convert to text

Speech is transcribed by browser speech recognition or a downloadable ASR model.

03

Estimate signals

Toxic-BERT estimates toxicity-related categories in the text.

04

Review responsibly

Read the context and record a human review; do not treat a score as a verdict.

ⓘ
Important limitation

This project uses a general toxicity classifier, not a definitive hate-speech detector. Sarcasm, reclaimed language, dialects, context, and non-English text may be misclassified. It must not be used for automatic punishment, censorship, or decisions about people.
